Domain watchdog

When no tune is running, it watches every hash domain. If one sits under 25% for 2 minutes, it pauses the chip for 5 seconds to bring it back (at most 3 times in 6 hours), and restarts the miner only if that fails.

Needs Solo45 firmware tune7 or later.
